Randomly Creating Character

Player will roll 3 six-sided dice, also known as a d6 . Use the total and use the table to record your modifier/bonus in your record sheet.

3d6 Roll STAT Bonus d6 chances (GM rolls behind screen) Spells per Day
3–5 -1 0-in-6 —
6–10 0 1-in-6 + d2 per day
11–14 +1 2-in-6 + d3 per day
15–17 +2 3-in-6 + d4 per day
18 +3 4-in-6 + d6 per day

Assign bonuses to:


      STR ength – Melee, brute force, lifting.

      AGI lity – Dodging, stealth, defense.

      PRE sence – Awareness, ranged attacks, magic.

      TOU ghness – Health, resist, withstand.

Rolling for Kin

Player will roll a d6 to generate what ancestry their character is. Kin will provide a flavor for players to use for roleplaying. This will offer a STAT bonus and maybe an ability.

Roll 1d6 Kin Bonus
1-2 Human +1 on any one STAT .
3 Stoneborn (Dwarf) +1 on TOU , a -1 on AGI .
4 Burrowfolk (Halfling) +1 on AGI , -1 on STR .
5 Firstborn +1 on PRE , -1 on TOU
6 Infernal +1 on PRE , -1 on TOU

Kin Description

Human

The youngest Kin and the most adaptable. They take what they want, and burn what they can’t use. +1 to any stat. No penalty. The old scriptures are gone. No one remembers where your kind came from, only that you found these lands... and now all others call you a plague.

Firstborn (Elf)

Ancient and strange. Their senses are sharp, their bodies frail. They remember too much. +1 PRE, -1 TOU. The Firstborn see all other Kin as lesser. They are beneath you. You are the closest thing to perfection, and you know it.

Burrowfolk (Halfling)

Small and fast. Clever. They live in the cracks where bigger things can’t reach. +1 AGI, -1 STR To survive, your kind forged new alliances, trading crops and cloth in exchange for peace. But the other Kin still see you as weak. Let them. They do not know how cunning or how charming your folk truly are.

Stoneborn (Dwarf)

Forged of stone and spite. Tough beyond reason, but slow to move and slower to trust. +1 TOU, -1 AGI Your ancestors built this plane. You do not forget. The Firstborn used your kin as labor, driving them deep into the dark. Let them walk proudly now, they stand on the bones of your forefathers.

Infernal

Fire-touched. Marked. Their very presence twists the air, but their bodies burn from within. +1 PRE, -1 TOU. You do not know when the unholy pact was struck, only that you bear its mark. Horns. Fangs. Skin red as clay. The villagers see you as an omen of the end. Let them. They will never understand the power that coils beneath your skin.

Gear

Use the following tables to generate your starting equipment:

  • Roll once for Bag .
  • Roll times D6, D8, D12 for Gear.
  • Roll twice for Weapons .
    • If you roll a non-Bulky weapon first, then roll a Bulky weapon second, you must choose one. If you keep the non-Bulky weapon, you may roll again for a second non-Bulky weapon.
  • Roll once for Armor .

Bag d4

Roll 1d4 Bag
1 No bag
2 Backpack (holds 7 items)
3 Sack (holds 10 items)
4 Small wagon and pick an extra bag from the table
