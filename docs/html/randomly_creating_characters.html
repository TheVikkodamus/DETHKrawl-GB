Randomly Creating Character

Player will roll 3 six-sided dice, also known as a d6. Use the total and use the table to record your modifier/bonus in your record sheet.

3d6 Roll STAT Bonus d6 chances (GM rolls behind screen) Spells per Day
3–5 -1 1-in-6 —
6–10 0 2-in-6 +d2 per day
11–14 +1 3-in-6 +d3 per day
15–17 +2 4-in-6 +d4 per day
18 +3 5-in-6 +d6 per day

Assign bonuses to:

      STRength – Smash, lift, hack, and slash.

      AGIlity – Dodge, sneak, escape from combat.

      PREsence – Spot, aim, cast a spell.

      TOUghness – Resist, heal, survive.
