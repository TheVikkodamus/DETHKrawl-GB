Randomly Creating a Character

Rolling for Stats

Each player rolls 3 six-sided dice (3d6) for each stat. Use the total to find your modifier in the table below, then record your bonus on your character sheet.

3d6 Roll STAT Bonus d6 Passive Chance Spells per Day
3–5−10-in-6—
6–1000-in-6—
11–14+11-in-6+ d2 per day
15–17+22-in-6+ d3 per day
18+33-in-6+ d4 per day

Assign bonuses to:

  • STRength — Melee, brute force, lifting.
  • AGIlity — Dodging, stealth, defense.
  • PREsence — Awareness, ranged attacks, magic.
  • TOUghness — Health, resist, withstand.

Rolling for Kin

Roll a d6 to generate your character's ancestry. Kin provides roleplaying flavor, a STAT bonus, and sometimes a special ability.

Roll 1d6 Kin Bonus
1–2Human+1 to any one STAT
3Stoneborn (Dwarf)+1 TOU, −1 AGI
4Burrowfolk (Halfling)+1 AGI, −1 STR
5Firstborn (Elf)+1 PRE, −1 TOU
6Infernal+1 PRE, −1 TOU

Setting

In this realm, the gods are fickle, magic is dangerous, monsters stalk the shadows, and untold treasure lies buried in crumbling ruins. The gods have died and returned so many times, they've forgotten who they were. Each resurrection leaves behind a trace of corruption, seeping into the land itself. The world is broken, senseless, and vain — and now we curse the gods for the ruin we call reality.

You'll crawl through cursed ruins and starving villages, and butcher your way through men and monsters alike. All of them want the same thing: to live one more day. Meet your kin who inhabit this ruined realm.

Kin Descriptions

Human

The youngest Kin and the most adaptable. They take what they want, and burn what they can't use. +1 to any stat. No penalty. The old scriptures are gone. No one remembers where your kind came from, only that you found these lands — and now all others call you a plague.

Firstborn (Elf)

Ancient and strange. Their senses are sharp, their bodies frail. They remember too much. +1 PRE, −1 TOU. The Firstborn see all other Kin as lesser. They are beneath you. You are the closest thing to perfection, and you know it.

Burrowfolk (Halfling)

Small and fast. Clever. They live in the cracks where bigger things can't reach. +1 AGI, −1 STR. To survive, your kind forged new alliances, trading crops and cloth in exchange for peace. But the other Kin still see you as weak. Let them. They do not know how cunning or how charming your folk truly are.

Stoneborn (Dwarf)

Forged of stone and spite. Tough beyond reason, but slow to move and slower to trust. +1 TOU, −1 AGI. Your ancestors built this plane. You do not forget. The Firstborn used your kin as labor, driving them deep into the dark. Let them walk proudly now — they stand on the bones of your forefathers.

Infernal

Fire-touched. Marked. Their very presence twists the air, but their bodies burn from within. +1 PRE, −1 TOU. You do not know when the unholy pact was struck, only that you bear its mark. Horns. Fangs. Skin red as clay. The villagers see you as an omen of the end. Let them. They will never understand the power that coils beneath your skin.

Gear

Use the following tables to generate your starting equipment:

  • Roll once on the Bag table (d4).
  • Roll once each on the d6 and d8 Gear tables.
  • Roll twice on the Weapons table (d12).
    • If your first roll is a non-Bulky weapon and your second is Bulky, you must choose one. If you keep the non-Bulky weapon, you may roll again for a second non-Bulky weapon.
  • Roll once on the Armor table (d6).
