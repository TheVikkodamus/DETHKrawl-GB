Core Mechanics

Core Resolution

  • Roll 1d20 + STAT modifier vs DR (Difficulty Rating).
  • If the result equals or exceeds the DR → success.
  • Default DR is 12, but it can shift based on the situation.

Difficulty Rating (DR)

When a player attempts something risky, the GM sets a Difficulty Rating (DR) for the task. The player rolls 1d20 + STAT modifier and compares it to the DR. If the total equals or exceeds the DR, the action succeeds.

  • 6 — Trivial
  • 8 — Routine
  • 10 — Skilled
  • 12 — Pressured
  • 14 — Tough
  • 16 — Heroic
  • 18+ — Nearly Impossible

Most checks are DR 12 unless the situation calls for something easier or more challenging. Let the fiction guide the number.

Stats

Each character has four stats:

  • STR — Strength: melee, brute force, lifting
  • AGI — Agility: dodging, stealth, defense
  • PRE — Presence: awareness, ranged attacks, magic
  • TOU — Toughness: survival, resistance, HP bonus

Roll 3d6 for each stat. Use the total to determine:

  • Your stat bonus (used in most rolls)
  • Your passive chance of success (d6 behind-the-screen rolls by the GM)
  • Your spells per day (for characters who can cast)
3d6 Roll STAT Bonus d6 Passive Spells per Day
3–5−11-in-6—
6–1002-in-6+ d2 per day
11–14+13-in-6+ d3 per day
15–17+24-in-6+ d4 per day
18+35-in-6+ d6 per day

Sometimes the GM will roll in secret to determine if a player notices or reacts to something without declaring an action — like spotting a trap, hearing footsteps, sensing danger, or intuiting a lie. Use the player's stat to determine their passive success chance on a d6.

Active vs Passive Abilities

  • If a player chooses to do something, they roll a standard test: 1d20 + STAT vs DR (usually DR 12).
  • If a player is unaware or not actively trying, the GM uses the passive d6 chance.

Examples of Passive Use:

  • AGI: Detecting a tripwire, reflexively dodging a sudden hazard
  • PRE: Hearing whispers behind a door, noticing someone's lying
  • TOU: Resisting fatigue, poison, or mind-altering fog
  • STR: Bracing against sudden collapse or holding a door shut

The GM rolls the d6 behind the screen and only informs the player if something is noticed, resisted, or if it's too late. If preferred, this roll can be made openly — by the GM or the player in front of everyone.

Advantage & Disadvantage

  • Advantage: Roll 1d20 + STAT + 2
  • Disadvantage: Roll 1d20 + STAT − 2

The GM applies these based on narrative positioning, tools, or environment.

GM Advice: Advantage and Disadvantage never stack. You're either favored or you're not.

Max Health

Starting MAX HP = 2d6 + TOU.

Death & Broken

Death lurks around every corner.

If HP drops to 0, roll 1d20 + TOU vs DR 12.

If HP drops below 0, the DethKrawler is dead. Another one takes their place.

  • Success = Broken (roll on the Broken Table)
  • Failure = Dead

GM Advice: Encourage each player to keep a pool of backup characters. When one falls, the next takes up the cause.

When Broken, roll a d6 to determine outcome (e.g., unconscious, wounded, or bleeding out). All rolls become DR 16 until treated.

Encumbrance

  • Carrying limit = 8 + STR slots
  • Bulky items = 2 slots
  • If encumbered: movement limited to Close; apply +2 DR to all AGI-based tests and Defense rolls.

DethKrawler's Die

You start with 1 DethKrawler Die per level (d6). You may spend one to:

  • Add to a Test or Damage roll
  • Block d6 incoming damage
  • Aid an ally

Cannot be used on a natural 1. You may gift one die to another player.

Omen

DethKrawl characters begin with d2 Omens. Spend an Omen to:

  • Reroll any die you rolled (keep the new result)
  • Reduce a test's DR by 4
  • Maximize damage on a successful hit
  • Neutralize a Crit or Fumble

Movement & Ranges

  • Ranges are abstract: Close, Mid, Far, Out of Range
  • You may move one range band per turn unless hindered.

Close

Melee — approx. 5 ft

Within 5 feet you can make melee attacks; both creatures are engaged.

Mid

Reach / Thrown / Spell Range — approx. 15 ft or as specified

Effective for reach weapons, thrown weapons, or short-range spells.

Far

Ranged line of sight — approx. 60 ft or greater

You may see but might not have a clean shot or range for all effects.

Out of Range

Beyond approx. 120 ft / Unaware

You can't meaningfully interact until you move closer or are alerted.

Recovery

Short Rest: Eat and drink → regain d4 HP

Long Rest: Eat, drink, and rest undisturbed → regain d6 HP

No food or water = no HP recovery. After 2 days without sustenance, lose d4 HP per day.

Spell Casting

Your PRE determines how many spells you can cast each day (see the Stat Table above).

When using sources with spell levels, a spell's DR increases by its level: L1 = DR 12, L2 = DR 13, etc.

Spell Dice Resolution

  • Roll 1d20 + PRE vs DR
  • If the result equals or exceeds the DR → success
  • Spellcasting DR is 12 unless modified by level or fiction

On a natural 1, you cannot use a DethKrawler Die — roll on the Mishap table.

Corruption

Whenever you commit an evil deed or roll a natural 1 while casting, gain 1 Corruption. At 10 Corruption, the GM takes control of the character.

Critical Miscast

On a natural 1 when spellcasting, gain +1 Corruption and roll 1d20 on the Miscast table.

How Many Spells Can I Cast Per Day?

At the start of the day, determine your casts from the Stat Table's Spells/Day entry tied to your PRE result.

How Can I Learn New Spells?

You may learn and retain a number of spells equal to PRE + Level (minimum 1). To learn from a powerful entity, succeed on a PRE test vs DR 18, then pay the price.
