Combat

Surprise Roll

Before combat or a tense encounter, the GM and the scouting player each roll a d6.

  • If the player rolls higher, they are not surprised and may act normally.
  • If the GM rolls higher, the player is surprised — they cannot act during the first round.
  • On a tie, both parties act cautiously — no one is truly surprised, but initiative proceeds as normal.
If no player is scouting or keeping watch, the GM rolls only and surprise is automatic unless the players succeed on a Passive PRE roll.
