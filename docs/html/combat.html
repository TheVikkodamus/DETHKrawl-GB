Combat

Surprise Roll

Before combat or a tense encounter, the GM and the scouting player each roll a d6.

  • If the player rolls higher, they are not surprised and may act normally.
  • If the GM rolls higher, the player is surprised — they cannot act during the first round.
  • On a tie, both parties act cautiously — no one is truly surprised, but initiative proceeds as normal.

If no player is scouting or keeping watch, the GM rolls only and surprise is automatic unless the players succeed on a Passive or Active PRE test roll.

Initiative

Dethkrawl is about fast combat. GM can decide if they would like to do PC vs GM roll using a 1d6 to see which group goes first. Whoever, rolls the highest they'll go first. Remeber GM will ask for declarations before rolling for initiative.

Quicker Combat

Before asking for player declarations at the beginning of the round, the GM rolls XdY — where X is the number of enemies in the encounter, and Y is the number of players at the table.

This determines which players the enemies will target during the round.

Once targets are set, each player rolls 2d20:

  • One die is the ATTACK roll
  • The other die is the DEFENSE roll

Resolve both rolls in the same beat. The GM narrates what happens based on both rolls, blending action and reaction into a single cinematic moment.
