# Tower of the Black Pearl
*A DethKrawl Dungeon Adventure for 3–5 Scum at Level 1*
Converted DCC/Goodman Games to Dethkrawl TTRPG. 

> “Once a decade, the moon drags back the tide — revealing a drowned spire of a mad wizard. Inside: _traps, treasure, and a pearl that can kill gods._”

---

## Setup

- **Adventure Hook**: The tide has receded. Tonight, Sezrekan’s drowned tower rises. You have 8 hours before the sea swallows it again.
- **Goal**: Retrieve the **Black Pearl** before time — or water — runs out.
- **Complication**: Savage Quenn and his pirates are already inside.

## Areas of the Map

### Area 1-1:
<a name:="1-1"></a>

> The tower rises from the sea like something out of a fairy tale. And yet, it must be real, for waves crash against it just as they lap at the planks of your skiff. Snarling gargoyles crown the four corners of the tower, peering from coral-encrusted battlements. More worrisome than the eldritch tower or the snarling gargoyles is the worn longboat moored on the far side of the tower. Squinting through the darkness, it seems that the deck of the longboat is empty.

![Area 1-1](https://i.imgur.com/jQPYg0D.png)

Three pirates are asleep atop the tower beside a keg of rum. Quenn left them with orders to guard the ship, but rum and poor discipline has rendered them senseless.

Any loud noise (like the PCs’ skiff knocking the side of the tower) wakes the pirates, who fight equally well drunk or sober. Given warning, the pirates make use of the cover afforded by the battlements, firing their bows at the PCs and trying to knock any boarders into the ocean.

## Monsters

### Pirate Ruffian
<a name="pirate-ruffian"></a>
HP 7, Morale 8, No Armor

**Short Sword**, d4 dmg

**Poisoned Knife**, d4 dmg + _**Special**_ PC. Test `TOU` DR10 or **become infected**. 

> An infected character does not benefit from resting. Instead, d6 HP is lost daily. 

### Animated Fetish
<a name="animated-fetish"></a>
HP 2, DR 14

Slam `d3–1`, Reduces all damage by 8

Immune to mundane weapons

### Crab-Rat
<a name="crab-rat"></a>

**HP** 4
 
**Defense**: No armor 

**Bite**: `d4+1` damage 

**Special – Diseased Bite**: 
When bitten, test `TOU` vs **DR12**. 

- **Fail**: Lose `d3` TOU each day until cured  
- **Effect**: All rolls made with **Disadvantage** while infected
- **Cure**: Magical healing or rest + successful healing test (GM’s call)
### Savage Quenn (Boss)
<a name="savage-quenn"></a>
HP 14, *Armor* -d4

**Rapier “Whisker”** `d6+2`, _causes 1 dmg bleed/round_

Can cast *Locate Object* 2x/day

## Arcane Artifacts

### The Black Pearl
<a name=  "black-pearl"></a>

- +1 DR to all Tests, and Spell Rolls
- Spells cast as if 1 level higher
- Triples wandering monster checks
- Attracts 5+ HD intelligent evil monsters

## Dark Age of Heroes

If Room 1-2 floods and the candles are extinguished:
- All Lawful NPC heroes die
- PCs gain +1 permanent Hit Die
- Divine magic falters across the world
- The world fractures into city-states
- You are now the only "heroes" left
