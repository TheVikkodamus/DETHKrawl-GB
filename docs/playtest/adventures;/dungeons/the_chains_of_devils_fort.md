# The Chains of Devil’s Fort
*A Cursed One-Shot for DethKrawl*

---

## Adventure Hook

You are no hero. But the **Inquisition** has summoned you:  
*A creature of dark renown has fled into the ruins of Devil’s Fort. Bring it back — alive or dead. Fail, and you may join the bones beneath its stones.*

But you are not the only ones hunting. A **cult of the Black Veil** approaches. If they reclaim the Fort, the land will suffer.

## Devil’s Fort

**Location:** Deep within Sarkash dark, past the Wailing Oaks.  
**Status:** Inactive — invaded.  
**Threat:** Cult forces arrive in `1d6 + 2` hours.  
**Inhabitants:** Hundreds of trapped skeleton warriors, restless maniacs, and the target creature.

---

## Entrance

**Locked Gate**  
- **DR14 Lockpicking** (Gear helps)  
- **STR DR16** to break  
- Guarded by **2 Dying Goblins**

---

## Rooms

### Room 1 — Feathers & Madness

- Floor strewn with **piles of black feathers**  
- **Smashed game board** — pieces twitch faintly  
- **Maniac spirit** attempts to **bind the mind**:  
    - **PRE DR14** to resist  
    - On fail: lose 1 action, **Disadvantage** `1d4` rounds

---

### Room 2 — Torture Chamber

- Rusted cages, twisted tools  
- **3 Vials of Life Elixir**:  
    - Restores `d6` HP or cures 1 Wound  
    - Drinking >1: **AGI DR14** or nausea (skip turn)

---

### Room 3 — Black Chrypt-fungi

- Thick cobwebs, dark fungus pulses on walls  
- Eating fungus:  
    - **Heals all HP**  
    - **+1 to all stats for 1 hour**  
    - Side effects:  
        - **d6**: 1–3 nothing; 4–5 hallucinations; 6 cursed (lose -1 PRE after)  
    - If hallucinating: **PRE DR12** each turn to act normally

---

### Room 4 — Rotten Chest

- **Heavy Chest** — **STR DR14** to open  
- Inside:  
    - 50 silver  
    - Cult sigil (arcane focus)  
    - Rotten planks + `d4` rusty nails → caltrops (**AGI DR12** to avoid)

---

## Wandering Threat: Restless Maniacs

- Mentally tied to Fort  
- Check every 10–15 min (**d6**):  
    - 1–2: 1 arrives  
    - 3–4: 1d3 arrive  
    - 5–6: none

---

## Main Objective: The Creature

- Found in collapsed **shrine** deeper in Fort  
- **Warped Cultist-Hybrid**  
    - HP 15  
    - Claws: `d8` melee  
    - Shriek: **PRE DR14** or Disadvantage 1 turn  
    - On kill: final Shriek — all in Close range must **PRE DR14** or become shaken

---

## Timer: Cult Arrival

**Cult arrives in `1d6 + 2` hours**

| Foes | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **d6 Cultists** | Light armor, `d6` dmg, fanatical |
| **Cult Leader (Warlock)** | HP 12, casts **Blast** (PRE DR12, `d8` dmg) |

---

## Escape Routes

- Back through entrance  
- Hidden passage from shrine (**PRE or AGI DR14** to locate)

---

## Treasure Ideas

- Black Chrypt-fungi samples (valuable but cursed)  
- Life Elixirs  
- Cult relic or sigil  
- Creature’s artifact — of heretical or holy value

---

## DethKrawl Stat Blocks

### Dying Goblin (x2)

- HP: **4**  
- **Knife**: `d4`  
- Desperate → fights to death unless spared

---

### Restless Maniac

- HP: **8**  
- **Rusted Blade**: `d6`  
- Immune to fear; will not retreat  
- On crit hit → target must **PRE DR12** or become shaken (Disadvantage 1 turn)

---

### The Creature — Warped Cultist-Hybrid

- HP: **15**  
- **Claws**: `d8`  
- **Shriek**: **PRE DR14** or Disadvantage 1 turn  
- Final Shriek on death → Close range, **PRE DR14** or shaken

---

### Cultist

- HP: **6**  
- **Dagger**: `d6`  
- Fanatical: fights to death

---

### Cult Leader (Warlock)

- HP: **12**  
- **Blast** (range, PRE DR12, `d8` dmg)  
- **Cursed Sigil**: if destroyed → leader takes `2d6` dmg
