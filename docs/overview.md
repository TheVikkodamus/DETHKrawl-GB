# DethKrawl – Project Overview

**DethKrawl** is a grim, classless dungeon-crawling OSR game built for the **Game Boy Color**, inspired by the aesthetics and mechanics of **Mörk Borg** and **Deathbringer**.

This document outlines the vision, structure, and core design goals for the game.

---

## Concept

> “You are not the chosen one. You are not even remembered. But the rot spreads — and someone must crawl into the dark.”

DethKrawl is a brutal turn-based dungeon crawler where players explore cursed crypts, collect randomized loot, and try to survive against overwhelming odds. It’s fast, dangerous, weird, and unfair — in true OSR fashion.

The game is classless. Your gear and stats shape your identity, and survival depends on smart choices, not leveling up.

---

## Inspirations

- **Mörk Borg** (metal-infused apocalyptic fantasy)
- **Deathbringer** (fast, lethal OSR micro-rules)
- **Pokémon & Dragon Warrior Monsters** (GBC-era interface)
- **Earthbound / Link’s Awakening** (exploration & charm)
- 80s VHS horror, doom metal, body horror, and weird fantasy

---

## Features

- Stat-based gameplay using **STR**, **AGI**, **PRE**, and **TOU**
- **8 points** to allocate at character creation
- Classless combat — your gear defines your role
- Randomized weapons, armor, and items
- D4/D6/D8 loot tables with scrolls, poison, relics, and gear
- 8 cursed dungeons, each with a distinct theme and boss
- Optional hotseat-style co-op (pass-and-play)
- Difficulty Rating (DR) system based on d20 rolls

---

## Setting

The game takes place in the region of **Irezar**, a dying land consumed by a spreading blight. Civilization is crumbling. Monsters breed in the cracks of ruined temples and forgotten vaults. Nothing can stop the decay — only delay it.

Each dungeon is a node of rot. A wound that festers. A truth best left buried.

---

## Development Goals

**Phase 1: Vertical Slice**
- Character creation
- 1 dungeon (4–5 rooms)
- 3 enemy types + 1 boss
- 1 loot table per slot (gear, weapons, armor)
- HTML5 and `.gb` export

**Phase 2: Full Game**
- All 8 dungeons
- Exploration maps between dungeons
- Dialogue with NPCs (cryptic or deranged)
- Scroll system with random effects
- Full sprite and tileset polish
