DethKrawl SRD

This game is inspired by Mörk Borg, Deathbringer, and the brutal glory of retro TTRPGs with a modern twist.

Welcome to our System Reference Document site! Here, you’ll find everything you need to run this game for free. You can also check out our books on Amazon, download the PDF from Itch.io, and visit our main site Kill the D20 for more awesome games!

This game is set in a world much like our own, during a twisted version of the 15th century. In this realm, the gods are fickle, magic is dangerous, monsters roam the land, and untold treasures lie in ruins waiting for someone to claim them. Players will delve into dungeons, explore crumbling kingdoms, and chase fortune before the world comes to an end. This is a rules-light, classless d20 system.

The tone is dark, gritty, and lethal. When a player character dies, another desperate soul will step over the corpse and leap into the chaos, eager to survive, grow rich, and avoid the same grisly fate.

This SRD will go over how to create a character, game mechanics, and more!

What is this for?

This game is for players who want to run a classless character, taking on dangerous jobs or quests far beyond their means. You are that desperate fool, seeking fortune while death watches closely for your failure. Should you fall, your soul is claimed, and your gear left behind for another unfortunate adventurer to plunder and steal your credit.

What You Need to Play

  • 1–5 Players + 1 Game Master (GM)
  • At least one 20-sided die (d20)
  • Have these handy: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12
  • Printed or digital character sheets (or scrap paper!)
  • Pencils, imagination, and a sense of doom
