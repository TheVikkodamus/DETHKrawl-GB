DethKrawl - Tabletop Role Playing Game

System Reference Document

This game is inspired by Mörk Borg by Free League Publishing, Deathbringer by Dan Masters (Professor DM), and the rotting glory of retro TTRPGs with a modern twist.

Welcome to the System Reference Document for DethKrawl TTRPG! Here, you’ll find everything you need to run this game for free. You can also check out our book on Amazon, download the PDF from Itch.io, visit Drive-Thru RPG, or explore our main site Kill the D20 for more grim and brutal games!

What Is This?

DethKrawl is a rules-light, stat-gritty dungeon crawler soaked in black powder smoke, corpse rot, and forgotten curses. There are no classes. Your gear, stats, and luck define your legend, or your death.

You are no hero, just another corpse that hasn’t hit the ground. When you fall, another fool will step over your bones to take your place.

What You Need to Play

  • 1–5 Players + 1 Game Master (GM)
  • At least two 20-sided dice (d20), preferably different colors
  • Other dice: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12
  • Printed or digital character sheets (or scrap paper!)
  • Pencils, imagination, and a sense of doom

Game Setting

The game is set in a world much like our own, during a twisted version of the 15th century. In this realm, the gods are fickle, magic is dangerous, monsters stalk the shadows, and untold treasures lie in the ruins, waiting to be claimed. Players will delve into dungeons, explore crumbling kingdoms, and chase fortune before the world devours them. This is a rules-light, classless d20 system.

The tone is dark, gritty, and lethal. When a player character dies, another desperate soul will step into the fray, eager to survive, grow rich, and avoid a similar grisly fate.

What Is This For?

This game is for players who want to run a classless character, taking on jobs or quests that are far beyond their means. You are that desperate fool, seeking fortune while death watches closely for your failure. Should you fall, your gear is left behind, and your soul is claimed, ready for another unfortunate adventurer to steal your glory and plunder your remains.
