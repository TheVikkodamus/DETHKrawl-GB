DethKrawl SRD

This game is inspired by Mörk Borg, Deathbringer, and the brutal glory of retro TTRPGs with a modern twist.

Welcome to our System Reference Document site! Here, you’ll find everything you need to run this game for free. You can also check out our books on Amazon, grab the PDF on Itch.io, and explore our home site Kill the D20 for more awesome games!

This game is set in a world similar to our own, during a fictionalized 15th century. Gods are fickle, magic is dangerous, monsters plague the land, and countless treasures await discovery — and quick spending. Players will delve into dungeons, explore crumbling kingdoms, and chase riches before the world comes crashing down. This is a rules-light, classless d20 system.

The tone is dark, gritty, and lethal. When a player character dies, another poor soul will step over their corpse and leap into the chaos, desperately trying to survive — and get rich — before suffering the same fate.
